- [How to be a product focussed engineer](https://jmoseley.github.io/presentations/product-focussed-engineer/)

The product engineer talk opens with Jeremy's introduction and photo. Its company
logos are stored locally in `site/product-focussed-engineer/assets/`, sourced from
[GenSX](https://www.gensx.com/logo.svg),
[Convoy](https://convoy.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Convoy_logo-white.svg),
[ClassDojo](https://static.classdojo.com/img/classdojo-logo.svg), and
[Inkling](https://www.inkling.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Inkling_LogoWeb-1.png).
Company logos remain the property of their respective owners.
The Convoy asset is cropped to its wordmark. ClassDojo uses a monochrome treatment
that preserves the mascot's facial details.

GitHub Pages deploys the contents of [`site/`](site/) when changes land on `main`.
